Enterprise AI Security & Compliance Guide

🔒 1. Enterprise Zero-Data Retention (ZDR)

What it means: Standard consumer AI tools (like free ChatGPT) may store user prompts on public servers to train future AI models. Zero-Data Retention guarantees that your prompts, documents, and output data are processed in memory and immediately deleted—they are never saved on server disks and NEVER used to train AI models.

💡 Managerial Value: Enables team members to safely input internal status notes, project specifications, and code snippets without risking company trade secrets or IP leaks.

🛡️ 2. SOC 2 & ISO 27001 Aligned

What it means: SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 are the premier global gold standards for information security management. They certify that a software system undergoes rigorous annual third-party audits evaluating data encryption, access control, vulnerability testing, and disaster recovery.

💡 Managerial Value: Ensures your corporate Information Security (InfoSec) and IT compliance departments can instantly approve your team's AI tool stack without security red tape.

🏥 3. HIPAA Compliant Frameworks

What it means: The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) mandates strict privacy controls for Protected Health Information (PHI). A HIPAA-compliant AI framework operates under a signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with mandatory end-to-end encryption and audit logging.

💡 Managerial Value: Allows healthcare administrators and clinical operations managers to automate administrative scheduling, audit prep, and clinical paperwork without risking federal compliance violations or patient privacy fines.
